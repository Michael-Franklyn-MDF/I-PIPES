HOW IT WORKS

From policy to insight in three steps

01
Define the Policy

Administrators configure ICT policies in the system, assigning relevant proxy indicators and weighting each one based on its importance to the policy's objectives.

02
Input & Analyse Data

Policy analysts input data against each indicator using trusted sources such as ITU, World Bank, or national statistics bureaus. The system processes inputs through a weighted scoring model.

03
Generate Impact Score

The system produces an overall impact score and a dimension breakdown — giving policymakers and researchers a clear, evidence-based picture of policy performance.
